# Part of HART. Mod Pack

HardModePatch 0.5.0 is now integrated into HART. Mod Pack 1.1.0. Use `DESCRIPTION.bbcode.txt` for the old item. The published 0.5.0 DLL and its source remain unchanged for compatibility and reproducible historical builds. Do not publish a second active patch release.

Settings now live on the one HART. Mod Pack page; reapply the series preset and personal choices there. Thriving Rivals and City Performance remain separate required items.
